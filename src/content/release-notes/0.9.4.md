## Handy Remote

This fork keeps Handy desktop and adds a remote mobile client. Pair over the
local network, use your phone as a mic, and send audio into the same models and
history that already run on your computer.

## Streaming vs Final Transcription

Streaming chunks are for live preview only. When a recording finishes, Handy
Remote finalizes the full audio and stores that transcription in history.
Reprocess / retranscribe uses the complete recording, not the preview chunks.

## Background Recording

Android can keep recording while the app is in the background. Grant the
notification permission so the OS can show the ongoing recording indicator.
