## Handy Remote

Mobile history, recording, and startup polish on top of Handy 0.9.5.

History shows real clock times and audio duration instead of `00:00`. Finishing a phone recording uploads one continuous file, so playback no longer cuts and fades between streaming chunks. Streaming stays for live preview only.

## Mobile

- Toggle recording start/stop sounds in settings
- Splash and system UI stay dark (no pink bar / white flash on launch)
- Already-granted microphone permission is reused when reconnecting a computer
- Model language filter shows the translated label
